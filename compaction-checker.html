How the checker works

Each row of the table is checked against your own entries, so one slip does not turn the whole row red: the dry unit weight should follow from your unit weight, and the zero-air-void unit weight from the moisture content and Gs.

The reference curve is a smooth curve of best fit with its wet leg parallel to an air voids line, as AS 1289.5.1.1 describes. A curve drawn by hand peaks at a slightly different place, so the maximum dry unit weight is accepted within ±0.15 kN/m³ and the optimum moisture content within ±0.6%. The air content is checked against your own γd max and wopt. The density ratio in the field check is checked against your own γd max.

The smooth curve on the plot is a cubic spline through your points, one of the methods the standard allows. Untick it to draw the curve yourself on paper.
