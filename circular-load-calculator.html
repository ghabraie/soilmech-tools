Under the centre, Δσz/q = 1 − 1/[(R/z)² + 1]3/2. At other points there is no simple formula; the calculator integrates the Boussinesq solution over the loaded circle numerically, as (1/2π)∫[F(ρ₁) − F(ρ₂)]dθ with F(ρ) = (z/√(ρ² + z²))³, where ρ₁ and ρ₂ are the distances along a ray from the point to where it enters and leaves the circle.
