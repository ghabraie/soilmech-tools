Plan view. The loaded area is shaded grey with a heavy outline. Each numbered rectangle runs from the point to one corner of the loaded area: solid blue ones are added, dashed red ones subtracted. Tap or drag to move the point.

Full table of corner influence factors Irec

Same values as the corner table in the book: rows are n = L/z, columns are m = B/z; the table is symmetric in m and n. Cells used in the current calculation are highlighted when m and n fall exactly on the table. Scroll sideways on narrow screens.

For a point under a corner, Δσz = q·Irec(m, n) with m = B/z and n = L/z. For any other point, the loaded area is replaced by four rectangles that each have the point as a corner and one corner of the loaded area as the opposite corner. Each is added or subtracted so that together they cover the loaded area exactly once: Δσz = q Σ (±) Irec(mi, ni).
