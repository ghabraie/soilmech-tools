How the checker works

The checker solves the problem from the given quantities using the relationships in this chapter, for example Se = wGs, γd = Gsγw/(1 + e) and γd = γ/(1 + w), and compares each of your answers with its own. A wrong answer gets a question about the relationship to use, and the checker also looks for common slips: e and n swapped, multiplying by (1 + w) instead of dividing, a decimal entered where a percentage is expected, density and unit weight mixed up, and the saturated unit weight used for a soil that is not saturated.

The phase diagram is the tool behind all these relationships: choose a convenient basis (a total volume of 1 m³, a volume of solids of 1 m³, or the actual sample), fill in what you know, and work out the rest. It uses γw = 9.81 kN/m³.
