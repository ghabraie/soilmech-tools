How the chart works

The plasticity index PI = LL − PL is plotted against the liquid limit LL. Casagrande's A-line, PI = 0.73(LL − 20), separates clays (above) from silts (below). The U-line, PI = 0.9(LL − 8), is an approximate upper bound for natural soils; a point above it usually means the test results should be checked.

Vertical lines on the liquid limit divide each group by plasticity:

  • USCS: low plasticity (L) for LL < 50 and high plasticity (H) for LL ≥ 50. Group names: lean clay (CL), fat clay (CH), silt (ML), elastic silt (MH).
  • AS 1726: clays are low (CL) for LL ≤ 35, medium (CI) for 35 < LL ≤ 50 and high (CH) for LL > 50; silts are low (ML) for LL ≤ 50 and high (MH) above.

Soils plotting above the A-line with 4 ≤ PI ≤ 7 fall in the hatched CL-ML zone (silty clay). Soils with PI < 4 are classed as silts. Organic soils use the same zones with the symbol O.

The shrinkage limit is estimated by drawing a line from the point to the intersection of the A-line and U-line at (LL, PI) = (−43.5, −46.4). The line crosses PI = 0 at the estimated shrinkage limit.
